# Requirements: v2.0 The Merchant's Odyssey

## Port Content & World Building

- [x] **PORT-01**: Each port displays rich historical lore — culture, customs, religion, daily life, and what the region is known for
- [x] **PORT-02**: Regional trade goods are historically accurate (Tyrian purple from Sidon, grain from Egypt, olive oil from Athens, silver from Iberia, cedar from Byblos)
- [x] **PORT-03**: Player can read "Port Lore" sections teaching real ancient history (Phoenician alphabet, Athenian democracy, Egyptian grain trade)
- [x] **PORT-04**: World expanded to 15-25 historically important ports (Tyre, Sidon, Corinth, Syracuse, Massalia, Gades, Memphis, Knossos, etc.)

## Quest & Narrative

- [x] **QUEST-01**: Main storyline begins at Piraeus with a commission from an Athenian merchant house
- [x] **QUEST-02**: Quests involve historically plausible merchant activities (deliver goods, source materials, negotiate deals)
- [x] **QUEST-03**: Completing quests advances the story and teaches history through gameplay
- [ ] **QUEST-04**: Win condition exists — completing the merchant's odyssey across the Mediterranean

## NPCs & Dialogue

- [x] **NPC-01**: Named NPCs exist at ports who give quests, share lore, and advance the story
- [x] **NPC-02**: NPC dialogue is historically grounded and educational
- [x] **NPC-03**: NPCs reveal new trade routes and ports through conversation
- [x] **NPC-04**: 5 key NPC characters with portraits displayed during dialogue

## Progressive Unlocking

- [x] **UNLOCK-01**: Player starts with Athens + 2 nearby ports, more unlock through trade and NPC introductions
- [x] **UNLOCK-02**: Port unlocking feels earned — tied to reputation, quest progress, or NPC referrals
- [x] **UNLOCK-03**: Locked ports visible on map but marked as unknown/unexplored

## Reputation & Standing

- [x] **REP-01**: Player builds trade reputation through successful trades and quest completion
- [x] **REP-02**: Reputation affects trade prices (better deals at ports where you're known)
- [x] **REP-03**: Reputation never gates required content — only modifies experience

## Captain's Journal Integration

- [ ] **JRNL-01**: Quest events, NPC conversations, and story milestones logged in captain's journal
- [ ] **JRNL-02**: Educational "discoveries" recorded (first time learning about a culture or trade good)
- [ ] **JRNL-03**: Journal becomes a personal history record by end of game

## Game Polish

- [ ] **POLISH-01**: Intro sequence at Piraeus sets the scene and introduces the story
- [ ] **POLISH-02**: Historically accurate voyage events expanded beyond current 24
- [x] **POLISH-03**: Port-specific ambient descriptions (sights, sounds, smells a merchant would experience)

## Future Requirements (deferred)

- Political faction system with reputation per city-state
- Ship progression and crew management
- Commodity chains (copper + tin = bronze)
- Teacher dashboard or progress export
- Bundle fonts for offline use

## Out of Scope

- AI-generated imagery — all visuals are public domain historical art or curated illustrations
- Multiplayer or online features — single-player educational experience
- Mobile/tablet versions — desktop Electron only
- Real-time gameplay — strictly turn-based
- Full voice acting — text-based dialogue only
